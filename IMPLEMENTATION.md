## Merchant content and operational dependencies

Shipping and return policies in the captured source conflict. The new purchase area links to the existing policy and adds no guessed threshold, fee, delivery window, or free-shipping promise. Confirmation is required before launch; retained policy pages still need reconciliation.

The founder portrait, actual maker names, workshop provenance, making time, on-body bag photography, customer images, and permissions must come from the brand. No substitute people or testimonials were created.

Wellness descriptions use catalog blend notes, not approved ingredient labels. Captured gram/millilitre discrepancies need product-label verification. Professional review is required before commercial wellness claims are published. Removing claims does not establish regulatory compliance. See [FDA cosmetic labeling claims](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetics-labeling/cosmetics-labeling-claims).

This remains a local storefront prototype with captured stock and browser-session cart behavior. A live checkout, live inventory, customer accounts, newsletter service, event registration, consented analytics, Search Console, and Merchant Center require service connections. No fake subscription form or empty event registration was added. The wishlist stays in the current browser.

Hero inventory and margin validation, merchant approval of the edit, creator campaigns, customer content collection, and launch measurement remain operational work. The static build is prepared for GitHub Pages but has not been published in this revision.
