## Applied to Dimohe

The split hero, four category tiles, four-product edit, and three craft cards remain. Colours and original photography remain. The hero now introduces cushion covers; the product edit pairs Gulnaar, Cypress Row, Cypress Bloom, and Terracotta Bloom. Selected product cards use a shorter collection name with an explicit product type and colour below it. Full names remain on product pages and in the shopping bag.

Close-up photos show the objects being discussed, not fictional artisans or workshop scenes. Ten product introductions have individual notes based on captured descriptions. Five cushion pages have explicitly selected complementary products. Craft copy names the visible details of Gulnaar, Noor Bagh, and Desert Pearl.

No competitor text, photographs, product names, promotions, impact claims, or services were copied. No bestseller or new-arrival label was added without sales or release evidence. The founder portrait is still awaiting the merchant’s image.
