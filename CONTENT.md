# Dimohe editorial direction, October 5, 2026

The collection connects Indian craft with everyday use. Keep the existing ivory, olive, and terracotta palette, original photography, and serif typography. Write simply and let the products carry the mood.

The homepage starts with “Block-printed linens. Hand-embroidered bags.” Shopping category tiles and category-specific Our Picks follow. The original 96-second Dimohe film bridges shopping and the Lucknow/Jaipur craft stories, preserving its portrait framing. The founder introduction remains separate. The palette, original photography, and restrained product motion are retained. Our Stories includes the journal alongside crafts and collection stories.

The edit is an initial merchandising selection, not a claim about sales, margin, or current live stock. Collector bags are grouped by the captured prices at $500 and above. Saved pieces use browser storage, without accounts or email collection.

Use short, specific sentences. Explain the technique and where it appears in the collection. Avoid abstract slogans about stories, rituals, intention, or moments. Do not use em dashes, en dashes, or double hyphens in visible copy. Retained source descriptions are normalized at presentation time; product identifiers and variant values are preserved.

## Craft discovery

Product departments remain the primary navigation. “Shop by craft” is a parallel path in the hamburger menu and desktop navigation. It leads to three collections: hand-block printing, Chikankari, and Zardozi. Product-type filters then narrow a craft collection. The dedicated craft story page explains the techniques and links to their products; relevant product pages link back to their craft traditions.

Home navigation groups its product types under sofa, table, and bath. The homepage product edit brings together Gulnaar, Cypress Row, Cypress Bloom, and Terracotta Bloom. Five cushion pages offer specific pairing notes and links to the selected complementary products. Craft cards use close-up gallery images and explain the visible details of Gulnaar, Noor Bagh, and Desert Pearl.

Ten individual product introductions use details from their captured descriptions. The original descriptions remain available in the product accordion. The About and founder pages are shorter, with no invented quotation, personal memory, or artisan biography. Reference observations and their applications are recorded in COMPETITOR-NOTES.md.

Membership is derived from captured descriptions and titles. A Zardozi product does not automatically become a Chikankari product. There are 46 hand-block printing products, 10 Chikankari products, and 11 Zardozi products; products can use more than one technique. Everyday rituals remain in Wellness rather than being labeled as a craft technique.

## Naming

Keep established collection names. Refine generic wording without hiding the product type or inventing a material, origin, or technique. Examples:

- Boy 3 Pc Royal Zardozi Bundy Set - White → Royal Zardozi Bundi Set · Pearl White
- Boy Dhola Maru Embroidery Bundy Set - Beige → Dhola Maru Embroidered Bundi Set · Sand Beige
- Royal Cypress Table Cover → Royal Cypress Tablecloth
- Girl Striped Fancy Zari Embroidered Top with Cowl Dhoti Pants- Minty Green → Zari-Embroidered Top & Cowl Dhoti Set · Mint Green

Original names remain in merchandising metadata and source catalog data. Shopify handles, variant identifiers, stock, prices, and option values are preserved. Basic color families remain in filters so discovery stays practical.
